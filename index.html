001 / AVA

Persian, English and Russian,
offline.

AVA is a desktop dictation app for Persian (fa-IR), English and Russian. It sits on the desktop as a single floating orb, records locally, recognises speech on your own computer and types the result straight into the focused window. No audio leaves the machine.

003 / Privacy & licenses

Nothing leaves
the machine.

// Local processing
  • 100% offline. All audio processing and transcription run on your local machine.
  • Total privacy. Voice data and text are never sent to cloud servers or third-party APIs.
  • Volatile memory. Audio chunks are processed in RAM and discarded right after typing.
  • One network call. The only outbound request is the one-time model download.
