What it is

AVA is a desktop dictation app for Persian (fa-IR), English and Russian. It sits on the desktop as a single floating orb, records locally, recognises speech on your own computer and types the result straight into the focused window. No audio leaves the machine.

Automatic language detection, four models

Auto is the default card: it identifies the language on every phrase on this PC and sends the audio to the matching offline model.

ModelLanguageSizeSpeed
Koochik (fast)Persian~0.45 GBabout 5× faster than real time
Nemotron (accurate)Persian~2.5 GBslower than real time on a CPU
Parakeet (English)English~670 MBabout 1 s per phrase
GigaAM (Russian)Russian~225 MBabout 0.3 s per phrase

In Auto the English model is a smaller Parakeet 110M (~465 MB, ~0.1 s per phrase) and the detector itself is Whisper base, used only to choose the language and never to transcribe. Models are downloaded once, on first use, and cached locally; after that AVA works fully offline.

Privacy & local processing

  • 100% Offline. All audio processing and transcription are executed entirely on your local machine.
  • Total Privacy. Your voice data and text are never transmitted to any cloud servers or third-party APIs.
  • Volatile Memory. Audio chunks are processed in RAM and discarded immediately after typing.
  • One network call. The only outbound request is the one-time model download from Hugging Face.

Third-party licenses

AVA runs fully offline and incorporates the following open-weights models and libraries. Model weights are downloaded on first use, not bundled, and keep their own licences. Application code is MIT.

  • Nemotron ASR Streaming Farsi (Persian, accurate) — mehdi-hf/nemotron-asr-streaming-farsi · OpenMDW License Agreement, v1.1
